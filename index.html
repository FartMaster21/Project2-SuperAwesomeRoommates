Roommates

Ricky

Ricky is 27 years old and a very open-minded, non-judgemental guy. He is gay and loves to party. He doesn't care about what role he takes in a relationship, he'll do whatever. He's a bit trusting to a fault and Dorian says he could use some paranoia and anxiety in his life. Ricky was the one who origninally recruited all the roommates via a poorly written Craiglist ad. He works part-time at the Foot Locker and part-time at a bar. He doesn't believe in one god, but three gods. Basharr, Rombus, and Kristian. Basharr looks like a traditional monk, Rombus is a very striking blue, and Kristian is hot pink. Every Sunday, Ricky goes to an alleyway in NY with a zen garden built in. Within this garden, there lies a pond and a small bowl. He's supposed to put $50 in the bowl and gently push it and let the pond carry it away. This ensures that he is protected and can proceed with the ascension (the afterlife for him). Every Thursday, he goes to his religion's version of mass where they tell everyone that if they don't keep donating $50, they will not get to the ascension.

At home, he is the positive roommate. He sees the good in both of his roommates and tries to keep the peace. He likes to decorate with pieces that may come across as tacky (especially to Dorian). Unfortunately, he can be quite messy. He leaves his dishes in the sink for too long sometimes and forgets to take out the trash. And his room? Oh boy, it's in horrible condition.

Dorian

Dorian is also 27 years old. His mom named him after Dorian Grey from the Oscar Wilde novel because of the homosexual undertones of the novel. At first, his mother thought she was bisexual, but after getting pregnant by her hubsand, she realized that men were not for her. Her husband also realized he was gay. Even though they realized this they remained married, therefore maintaining a lavender marriage. They decided to go with this route as they grew up in a conservative town. However, once they moved to New York, they had a very loose lifestyle that Dorian felt embarrassed and ashamed by. Even though his parents weren't religious, he took it upon himself as a child to become a devout Christian. For a very long he tried to convince himself he wasn't gay. However, he realized the church would hate him if they ever found out the true nature of his emotions, so he left. He does not like to do leg day at the gym.

Dorian met Ricky through a friend of a friend who sent him the Craiglist ad where Ricky was looking for roommates. He is a rather rigid and stuck up individual. He doesn't like how messy Ricky can be sometimes and hates it when he leaves the dishes in the sink or forgets to take out the trash. He also dislikes how often Ricky brings people over and how loud they can be at times. Not only that, but he believes that Ricky's sense of decor is rather tacky. He is scared of Grayson and doesn't really like him. Despite all of this, he stays because the rent is cheap and it's close to his job.

Grayson

This is Grayson Smith. Age unknown, date of birth unknown. All his roommates know is that he's agnostic and leaves at 7am and comes back home at 5pm. They don't know where he goes. He also occasionally leaves at 2 or 3 am and comes back before 7am. Again, they don't know where he goes. Grayson doesn't speak. He has a ghastly, almost sickly appearance. His skin is basically grey. Despite his appearance, he is a pretty nice guy. Much like his roommates he is gay. He likes men that are his exact height (which changes) so he can look them straight in the face without having to look up or down. Grayson really likes to people watch. It comes from a genuine place of curiosity and wonder. Not only that, but both of his eyes used to be the same size before one of them shrunk and impaired his vision. Therefore, he wants to make the most out of his good eye by observing the world as much as he can. He's a bit scared that one day his other eye will shrink and he won't be able to see at all.

He likes to watch his roommates sometimes. He's watched Dorian from the crack in his door, watching BTS videos (Dorian is a secret BTS fan, but doesn't want to admit it). Grayson is a good roommate. He takes out the trash, does his dishes, sometimes makes extra food for his roommates, and pays rent on time. He was the first guy to respond to Ricky's Craiglist ad.
